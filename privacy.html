Privacy Policy

Learn how Quick Image Tools handles information and browser-based image processing.

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Image files

The image tools on this website process selected image files locally in your browser. The files are not uploaded to our server for the purpose of image processing. Do not use a shared device for private files unless you trust that device.

Information collected by hosting and browser services

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser details and request logs as described in its own policies. Your browser may also store ordinary site data needed to load and use the website.

Cookies, advertising and third parties

At the time of this update, Quick Image Tools does not display Google AdSense advertisements. If advertising is enabled in the future, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalize advertisements based on a visitor’s prior visits to this website or other websites. These technologies may allow Google and its partners to show relevant advertisements, measure ad performance and limit repeated ads. The use of these technologies will be subject to the providers’ policies and applicable privacy and consent requirements.

Visitors can manage Google advertising personalization through Google Ads Settings. Information about how Google uses data on partner websites is available at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Visitors may also learn about opting out of some participating companies’ personalized advertising at AboutAds.info. When advertising is activated, this policy and any required consent messages will be reviewed and updated to reflect the actual ad providers and applicable requirements.

External links

This website may link to third-party services. We do not control their content or privacy practices. Review their policies before using them.

Children's privacy

This website is a general-purpose utility site and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the website changes. The updated date will be shown at the top of this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact method described on our Contact page. Please do not post personal or sensitive information publicly.
