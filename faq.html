Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about using Quick Image Tools.

Are the tools free?

The tools currently published on this website are available free of charge.

Are my images uploaded?

The image tools process files in your browser. They do not send the selected image to our server for processing.

Which formats are supported?

Support varies by tool and browser. The available tools cover JPG/JPEG, PNG and WEBP where supported by the browser.

Why did my file become larger?

Some formats and quality settings can produce a larger file. Try another quality setting or output format, and compare the result before downloading.

Will compression keep the original quality?

Lossy formats such as JPG and WEBP may reduce image quality as file size is reduced. Keep your original file and review the output before using it.

Can I use the tools on a phone?

Yes. The website is designed to work in modern mobile and desktop browsers. Very large images may take longer or exceed the memory available on some devices.

Where can I report a problem?

Visit our Contact page to find the issue tracker.

Can I use these tools on a phone?

Yes. Open the website in a recent mobile browser. Processing takes place on your device, so very large images may take longer or may not work if your phone has limited available memory.

Does resizing add detail to an image?

No. Increasing pixel dimensions creates a larger pixel grid but cannot recover detail that was not present in the original image. Reducing dimensions removes pixels, so keep a copy of the original.

Why does a converted image sometimes have a different background?

JPG does not support transparency. When a PNG with transparent areas is converted to JPG by these tools, those areas are filled with white.

Will my original image be changed?

No. The tool creates a separate result for you to download. Your original file on your device is not overwritten. Keep it until you have checked the new file.

Why can processing fail?

The file may be damaged, in a format your browser cannot open, or too large for the memory available on your device. Try a supported image file or a smaller copy.

Does the website guarantee AdSense approval?

No. Using the tools does not guarantee any search ranking, traffic or advertising approval. Advertising providers review websites under their own policies and requirements.
